Theorem. Linear dependence lemma.
Try to state it, then click to flip
Theorem. Let
  • $V$ be a vector space over $\mathbb F$.
  • $v_1,\ldots,v_m$ be a linearly dependent list in $V$.
Then there exists $k\in\{1,\ldots,m\}$ such that$$ v_k\in\operatorname{span}(v_1,\ldots,v_{k-1}). $$ Furthermore, if $k$ satisfies this condition, then$$ \operatorname{span}(v_1,\ldots,v_{k-1},v_{k+1},\ldots,v_m)=\operatorname{span}(v_1,\ldots,v_m). $$
NoteFor $k=1$, the condition says $v_1=0$, since $\operatorname{span}()=\{0\}$.
Definition. Basis.
Try to state it, then click to flip
Definition. Let
  • $V$ be a vector space over $\mathbb F$.
A basis of $V$ is a list of vectors in $V$ that is linearly independent and spans $V$.
Theorem. Dimension of a sum.
Try to state it, then click to flip
Theorem. Let
  • $V$ be a finite-dimensional vector space over $\mathbb F$.
  • $V_1,V_2$ be subspaces of $V$.
Then$$ \dim(V_1+V_2)=\dim V_1+\dim V_2-\dim(V_1\cap V_2). $$
NoteIn particular, if $V_1\cap V_2=\{0\}$, then $\dim(V_1\oplus V_2)=\dim V_1+\dim V_2$.
