Definition. Null space, $\operatorname{null} T$.
Try to state it, then click to flip
Definition. Let
  • $V$ and $W$ be vector spaces over $\mathbb F$.
  • $T\in\mathcal L(V,W)$.
The null space of $T$, denoted $\operatorname{null} T$, is$$ \operatorname{null} T=\{v\in V:Tv=0\}. $$
NoteThe null space is also called the kernel.
Theorem. Fundamental theorem of linear maps.
Try to state it, then click to flip
Theorem. Let
  • $V$ be a finite-dimensional vector space over $\mathbb F$.
  • $W$ be a vector space over $\mathbb F$.
  • $T\in\mathcal L(V,W)$.
Then $\operatorname{range} T$ is finite-dimensional and$$ \dim V=\dim\operatorname{null} T+\dim\operatorname{range} T. $$
Theorem. Dimension of a quotient space.
Try to state it, then click to flip
Theorem. Let
  • $V$ be a finite-dimensional vector space over $\mathbb F$.
  • $U$ be a subspace of $V$.
Then$$ \dim V/U=\dim V-\dim U. $$
NoteEquivalently, $\dim V=\dim U+\dim V/U$.
