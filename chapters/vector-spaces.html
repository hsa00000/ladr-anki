Definition. Vector space.
Try to state it, then click to flip
Definition. Let
  • $\mathbb F$ be a field, either $\mathbb R$ or $\mathbb C$.
  • $V$ be a set.
  • $+:V\times V\to V$ be a function.
  • $\cdot:\mathbb F\times V\to V$ be a function.
Then $V$ is a vector space over $\mathbb F$ if all of the following hold:
1. $\forall u,v\in V,\ u+v=v+u$.
2. $\forall u,v,w\in V,\ (u+v)+w=u+(v+w)$.
3. $\forall a,b\in\mathbb F,\ \forall v\in V,\ (ab)v=a(bv)$.
4. $\exists 0\in V,\ \forall v\in V,\ v+0=v$.
5. $\forall v\in V,\ \exists w\in V,\ v+w=0$.
6. $\forall v\in V,\ 1v=v$.
7. $\forall a\in\mathbb F,\ \forall u,v\in V,\ a(u+v)=au+av$.
8. $\forall a,b\in\mathbb F,\ \forall v\in V,\ (a+b)v=av+bv$.
NoteThe function $+$ is called addition, and $\cdot$ is called scalar multiplication.
Definition. Direct sum.
Try to state it, then click to flip
Definition. Let
  • $V$ be a vector space over $\mathbb F$.
  • $m$ be a positive integer.
  • $V_1,\ldots,V_m$ be subspaces of $V$.
The sum $V_1+\cdots+V_m$ is a direct sum if$$ \forall v\in V_1+\cdots+V_m,\quad \exists!\,(v_1,\ldots,v_m)\in V_1\times\cdots\times V_m:\ v=v_1+\cdots+v_m. $$ In that case the sum is denoted$$ V_1\oplus\cdots\oplus V_m. $$
Theorem. Condition for a direct sum.
Try to state it, then click to flip
Theorem. Let
  • $V$ be a vector space over $\mathbb F$.
  • $m$ be a positive integer.
  • $V_1,\ldots,V_m$ be subspaces of $V$.
Then $V_1+\cdots+V_m$ is a direct sum if and only if$$ \forall (v_1,\ldots,v_m)\in V_1\times\cdots\times V_m,\quad v_1+\cdots+v_m=0\Longrightarrow v_1=\cdots=v_m=0. $$
