Definition. Determinant of an operator, $\det T$.
Try to state it, then click to flip
Definition. Let
  • $V$ be a finite-dimensional vector space over $\mathbb F$.
  • $T\in\mathcal L(V)$.
The determinant of $T$ is$$ \det T:=\begin{cases}1,&\dim V=0,\\\text{the unique }c\in\mathbb F\text{ such that }\alpha_T=c\alpha\ \forall\alpha\in V_{\mathrm{alt}}^{(\dim V)},&\dim V>0.\end{cases} $$
NoteFor $\dim V>0$, existence and uniqueness follow because $\dim V_{\mathrm{alt}}^{(\dim V)}=1$.

Theorem. Let
  • $V$ be a finite-dimensional vector space over $\mathbb F$.
  • $T\in\mathcal L(V)$.
  • $q$ be the characteristic polynomial of $T$.
Then$$ q(T)=0. $$

Definition. Let
  • $V$ and $W$ be finite-dimensional vector spaces over $\mathbb F$.
The tensor product $V\otimes W$ is$$ V\otimes W:=\mathcal B(V',W'). $$
