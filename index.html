Theorem. Complex spectral theorem.
Try to state it, then click to flip
Theorem. Let
  • $V$ be a nonzero finite-dimensional complex inner product space.
  • $T\in\mathcal L(V)$.
Then the following are equivalent.
1. $T$ is normal.
2. $\exists$ orthonormal basis $e_1,\ldots,e_n$ of $V$ such that $\mathcal M(T,(e_1,\ldots,e_n))$ is diagonal.
3. There is an orthonormal basis of $V$ consisting of eigenvectors of $T$.

FAQ

Is this the book? No. It is an independent flashcard deck that follows the book's chapter order, and it is meant to be used with the book. The book itself is free from the author's website. The deck is not affiliated with or endorsed by Sheldon Axler or Springer.

What do the cards contain? Definitions and theorem statements with their hypotheses spelled out, plus a few short notes. They do not contain proofs or exercise solutions.

What do I need? Anki, which is free on Windows, Mac, Linux, Android, and the web (AnkiMobile for iPhone and iPad is a paid app). You also need an internet connection while reviewing, because the card styling and formulas load online.

Can I try before buying? Yes. Chapters 1–2 (75 cards) are free on AnkiWeb. Note that AnkiWeb's web preview cannot display the formulas, so open the deck in Anki to see the cards properly.
